feat(server): split backend into config/models/middleware/utils and add DOCX parser

- server/config/: env, constants, database (retry without dropping data).
- server/models/: user, question, exam, attempt, progress, import-job, audit-log.
  Question and Attempt never expose correctAnswer to the student API.
- server/middleware/: auth (server-side role decision only), admin, error,
  upload (.docx only, size + magic-byte checks), validation.
- server/utils/: logger, response envelope, sanitize, hash.
- server/parsers/docx/: pure-JS OOXML reader with no extra dependency.
  Handles "Câu 1." / "1." numbering, A. B) C) options, inline answers,
  end-of-document answer keys, explanations, embedded images and OMML
  formulas. Answers are NEVER guessed: unresolved answers stay null and
  block publishing.
- server/services/docx-import.service.js: orchestrates the parse pipeline.
- tests/fixtures/make-sample-docx.js: builds real DOCX files for tests.
